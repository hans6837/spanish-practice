Spoken test of the words and phrases you have practised in the course. Spanish → English: you hear the Spanish and say what it means in English. English → Spanish: you see (and hear) the English and say it in Spanish. Wrong answers come back after a minute; right answers come back later and later (10 minutes, then 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days).

“Both directions”: a word is first tested Spanish → English; once you get it right, English → Spanish is added.
